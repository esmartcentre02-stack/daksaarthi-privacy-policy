DAKSAARTHI Privacy Policy

Smart Booking Assistant · Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the DAKSAARTHI Smart Booking Assistant Android application (“DAKSAARTHI”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use it.

1. Information handled by the app

Depending on the features you use and the information you enter or import, DAKSAARTHI may process:

  • Article and booking information, including barcodes/article numbers, booking details, physical weight, COD type and amount, descriptions and remarks.
  • Receiver or customer information, including name, mobile number, postal address and PIN code.
  • Information in Excel files you choose to import, and information in Excel, PDF, label or report files you choose to generate or export.
  • Information needed to create, protect, restore or manage an encrypted app backup.
  • Google Play subscription and purchase status when you use subscription features.

Please enter or import only information you are authorised to use. Receiver/customer information may be personal information about another person.

2. How information is used

The app uses information to provide its core functions, such as preparing bulk-booking data, generating article labels and reports, managing receiver details, tracking articles, importing/exporting files, restoring backups and checking subscription access.

3. Where information is stored and shared

Article, receiver and booking information is designed to be stored locally on your device for the app’s functions. DAKSAARTHI does not operate its own app backend to upload this article/customer database as part of the standard local workflow.

If you export or share a file, open another app, or choose to send a message, information you select may be made available to the destination you choose. SMS, WhatsApp, email, printing and other sharing apps are separate services and their own privacy terms apply. Review the recipient and content before sharing.

When you purchase or restore a subscription, Google Play processes the transaction and provides subscription/purchase information to the app so it can determine subscription status. Google’s handling is governed by Google’s policies and terms.

Android device backup settings may affect whether app data is included in device-level backups. You control relevant Google/device backup settings.

4. Backups and security

The app provides a separate password-protected encrypted backup feature. Keep your backup password private and store backup files securely. If you forget the password, recovery may not be possible. Anyone with access to an unlocked or compromised device, an exported file, or a password you disclose may be able to access information.

The app uses device/app security features intended to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device, exported files and backup password.

5. Retention and deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it using available app functions, remove relevant files/backups, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Before uninstalling or clearing storage, export or back up anything you need. Deleting a file from the app may not delete copies previously exported, shared, printed or saved by another app. Subscription records are managed by Google Play under its applicable policies.

6. Permissions and external services

The app may request permissions needed for features you use, such as scanning barcodes with the camera. If you use Google Play billing, messaging, email, printing, mapping or another external service, that service may process information under its own privacy policy. Permission availability and behaviour can vary by Android version and device.

7. Children

DAKSAARTHI is a business/productivity tool and is not designed specifically for children. Do not knowingly provide children’s personal information through the app unless you are authorised to do so.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its practices change. The “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision. Please review this page periodically.
